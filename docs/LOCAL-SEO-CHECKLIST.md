## 4. Google Reviews Acquisition Workflow
- Never buy artificial reviews — Google actively removes them and can suspend listings.
- Instead, build a simple post-trip habit:
  1. Once the trip ends, send a WhatsApp:
     > *"Hello [Customer Name]! Hope you had a smooth and comfortable journey with Urgent Taxis. If our driver served you well, could you please take 30 seconds to drop us a quick 5-star review on Google? It means the world to our team: [Your Google Review Link]. Thank you!"*
  2. Always reply to every review within 24 hours thanking the customer by name.
